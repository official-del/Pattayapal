# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PattayaPal serves creators, freelancers, clients, and platform operators.

Creators and freelancers use the product to present their portfolio, publish work, build reputation, receive job requests, earn platform rewards, complete quests, and communicate with clients.

Clients use the product to discover reliable creators, compare portfolio quality, post or manage jobs, start conversations, and track hiring progress.

Operators and admins use the product to manage users, works, withdrawals, notifications, platform activity, and operational health.

## Product Purpose

PattayaPal is a creator marketplace and community workspace. It combines discovery, portfolio browsing, hiring, jobs, wallet, coin, gas, rank, quests, messenger, notifications, and admin workflows in one product.

Success means the interface feels beautiful, organized, trustworthy, and easy to scan while keeping the existing PattayaPal identity: dark surfaces, orange energy, creator/community personality, and lightweight game-like reward signals.

## Brand Personality

Playful, premium, creator-first.

The product should feel energetic and memorable without becoming chaotic. It should support a modern marketplace experience first, then use game or pixel-inspired accents only where they help the product language: badges, rank, coin, gas, quests, status, and portfolio highlights.

## Anti-references

PattayaPal should not look like a generic SaaS template, a plain corporate dashboard, or an over-decorated futuristic interface.

Avoid heavy glassmorphism, excessive glow, blurry decorative backgrounds, dense visual noise, and pixel styling so strong that Thai or English text becomes hard to read.

Avoid changing the brand into a completely new color identity. The redesign should keep the existing dark and orange theme while making the interface more polished, consistent, readable, and structured.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve identity while improving order.
Keep PattayaPal's dark and orange character, but reduce visual clutter and make hierarchy easier to understand.

2. Make creator work easy to judge.
Portfolio cards, profiles, and work details should put media, skills, rank, and hiring actions in a clear order.

3. Treat product workflows as real work.
Dashboard, jobs, wallet, messenger, and admin screens should prioritize scanning, status clarity, and predictable actions.

4. Use game signals with restraint.
Coin, gas, rank, and quest UI should feel native to PattayaPal, but should not overpower forms, tables, feed content, or portfolio browsing.

5. Design for Thai and English content.
Typography, spacing, and line-height must support mixed-language UI without crowding, overflow, or excessive letter spacing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast for text and important UI states.

Support reduced motion for major animations, loading states, hover effects, and page transitions.

Keep focus states visible, controls recognizable, and forms readable on mobile and desktop.

Avoid relying on color alone for status. Pair color with labels, icons, or clear state text.
